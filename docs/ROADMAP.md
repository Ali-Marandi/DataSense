# DataSense product roadmap

## 2026 product direction

DataSense is being developed as a local-first trusted analytics workbench. The product should make analysis repeatable, evidence-aware and safe to export without pretending that governance metadata proves scientific or causal validity.

### Foundation
- Data import/export across tabular formats and databases
- deterministic transformation history and versioning
- privacy-preserving profiling and Trust Center
- schema drift, contracts, quality gates and lineage
- reproducible statistical and ML analysis
- finance/risk analytics
- HTML/PDF/JSON evidence exports

### Analytics intelligence
- effect sizes, confidence intervals and multiple-testing controls
- leakage-aware ML evaluation and time-aware splits
- model fingerprints and environment metadata
- portfolio risk analytics and volatility diagnostics
- scenario analysis and sensitivity studies

### Product experience
- canonical analysis recipes
- reusable report templates
- evidence-aware dashboard
- saved/replayable analysis runs
- keyboard-first workflows and accessible states
- Persian/English localization with RTL support

### Enterprise hardening
- dependency/security policy enforcement
- artifact provenance and signed release evidence
- database least-privilege controls
- controlled external connectors
- role-aware approvals and audit trails
- performance budgets for large datasets and long-running jobs

### Commercial readiness
- focused vertical templates
- pilot onboarding and activation instrumentation
- install/upgrade reliability
- supportable release process
- documented limitations and domain-specific evidence boundaries
